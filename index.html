Quick climate facts

🌡️
What is a carbon footprint?

The total greenhouse gases caused by your activities, usually measured in tonnes of CO₂-equivalent.

✈️
Flights add up fast

A single long-haul return flight can produce more emissions than months of daily commuting.

💡
Energy is the big lever

Efficient appliances, LED bulbs and renewable power cut household emissions significantly.
